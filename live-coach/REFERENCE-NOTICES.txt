Importer: bounded LD header/channel traversal and unit-checked conversion.
Raw Lap Distance (metres) is used, never integrated speed or normalized
travelled distance. Input channels 10 Hz; distance/path-lateral 5 Hz. The
export keeps 545 actual samples at 5 Hz from 9.40125 to 5772.7755 m and
does not invent start/finish crossing samples. Partial coverage prevents
full-lap delta display. Clock alignment is the LD channel time axis, not
an independently measured sensor-latency correction.

GPS exists but its inverse mapping to native LMU world X/Z is not verified.
World X/Z are omitted. Path Lateral is preserved as an independent scalar;
it is not a reconstructed map or road edge, and its sign is not labelled as
driver-left/right. The installed LMU SDK describes mPathLateral as position
relative to a *very approximate* centre path. Our compatible reader takes
this double at scoring-row offset 112 (after mLapDist at 104). Synthetic
decoder tests check the offset and missing values. No SDK code is bundled.

The exact LD exporter was not identified. Its generic header device is ADL;
that does not establish a named logger. Native scoring and MoTeC basis IDs
remain distinct. Explicit exploratory alignment allows only the known LMU
pair, compatible known lengths or a near-complete partial recording covering
at least 98% of known native length. Unknown source length stays unknown.
Game/car/track, known layout, validity and physics-version gates still apply.
Warnings remain visible in the comparison evidence tab. Actual in-game
channel/time/lateral mapping and the final integrated UI are not yet verified.

Observations can identify where two recordings differ. Fuel, tyres, setup,
weather, version, traffic and clock/sampling uncertainty prevent those
differences alone from proving a driving-error cause. Sampling uncertainty
is an engineering estimate, not a guaranteed error bound.
